Something not working, or have a question about the game? Write to us at [{{ site.operator.email }}](mailto:{{ site.operator.email }}?subject=Stone%20Runner%20support). We usually reply within a few working days, in English or Hungarian.

It helps if you include:

- your device model and operating system version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 26),
- the game version (shown in the settings),
- what happened and what you expected, with a screenshot if you can,
- for a purchase problem: the order number. On iOS it is in the receipt email from Apple; on Android it starts with GPA. and is in the receipt email from Google Play.

## Common questions {#faq}

### I bought something but did not get it {#missing-purchase}

Close the game completely and open it again with an internet connection. The store sends unfinished purchases again, and the game credits them once. If it still does not arrive, write to us with the order number.

### How do I restore the Tribal Chief pack on a new device? {#restore}

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the shop and tap "Restore purchases". Consumable items, such as flint and gems, cannot be restored by the store.

### Can I move my progress to a new phone? {#new-device}

Your progress is stored only on your device; there is no online account or cloud save yet. If you move to a new phone of the same kind using a full device backup (for example iCloud backup or a device-to-device transfer), your progress usually comes with it.

### I watched a video but did not get the reward {#video-reward}

The reward is given when the advertising partner confirms that the video finished. If you closed the video early or the connection dropped, no reward is given, but you do not lose anything either. Daily limits also apply.

### How do I change my ad consent? {#consent}

Open the game's settings and choose Privacy. You can change or withdraw your consent there at any time.

### How do I get a refund? {#refund}

Refunds are handled by the store:

- App Store: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Google Play: [request a refund on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)

### How do I delete my data? {#delete-data}

Uninstall the game. This deletes your progress from the device; we do not keep a copy. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, just ask. More details are in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/stone-runner/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
