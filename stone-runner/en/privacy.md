## In short
{:.no_toc #in-short}

- There is no account or sign-up. Your game progress is stored only on your device.
- The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
- Video ads are optional and only play when you start them. They are not personalized, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
- We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not sell your data.

## Data stored on your device {#on-device}

The game saves your progress in a file on your device: your vehicles and saved builds, parts and accessories, in-game currency balances, settings, the IDs of purchases already credited (so the same purchase is never credited twice), daily counters for reward videos, and a random profile identifier generated by the game.

- This file stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and it does not contain your name or any contact details.
- If your device backup is turned on (for example iCloud or Google backup), your operating system may include this file in the backup, under Apple's or Google's terms.
- You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game. This also deletes your progress, which we cannot restore because we do not have a copy.

## In-app purchases {#purchases}

You can buy virtual items in the game (for example flint, gems or the Tribal Chief pack). Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your card or bank details, your name or your email address.

To deliver what you bought, the game receives from the store the product ID, the transaction ID, the date of purchase and a signed confirmation. The game checks the signature, credits the item and stores the transaction ID locally. The stores also send us sales reports, which do not identify you to us.

- Purpose: delivering and restoring your purchases, preventing double crediting and fraud.
- Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Retention: stored on your device as described above. Apple and Google keep their own records under their policies.

## Optional reward videos {#ads}

You can choose to watch a short video to earn an in-game reward. Videos never start on their own, and there are no banner or interstitial ads. The whole game can be played without watching any ads.

Videos are delivered through **Unity LevelPlay** (Unity Technologies), a mediation platform that selects an ad from advertising networks such as **Google AdMob**. To be able to offer a video, the advertising software starts when the game starts and connects to these partners. The partners may process:

- device and app information (device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, app version),
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level),
- device identifiers: on Android the advertising ID; on iOS the identifier for vendors. We do not ask for tracking permission on iOS, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to them,
- ad interaction data (which ad was shown, whether it was completed or tapped) and technical diagnostics.

**Purposes:** showing a video and confirming that you watched it so you get your reward, limiting how often the same ad appears, measuring ad delivery, and preventing fraud and abuse.

**No personalized ads.** We configure the advertising software to show non-personalized ads. The partners may still use contextual information, such as the language, the approximate location and the game itself.

**Legal basis:** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules) for storing or accessing information on your device and for processing for advertising. We ask for it in a consent form before the first video offer. If you decline, partners may only show limited ads that do not rely on your consent, or no video is offered. Processing strictly needed for security and fraud prevention is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Your choices:**

- You never have to watch a video.
- You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game's settings under Privacy. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.
- On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The advertising partners also act as independent controllers for some of their processing. You can read about it here:

- Unity: [Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)
- Google: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Other networks that take part in the mediation are listed in the consent form, where you can also see their privacy policies.

## When you contact us {#contact-data}

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message (for example your device model or a purchase ID).

- Purpose: answering your question and solving the problem.
- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing support (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); for a question about a purchase, the performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Retention: until your request is closed, then for at most 1 year, unless we need it longer to establish or defend legal claims.
- Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages on our behalf.

## What we do not do {#we-do-not}

- We do not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
- We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the game.
- We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- The game only uses the network permissions needed for purchases and videos. On Android, the advertising software also declares the advertising ID permission, used as described above.

## Children {#children}

Stone Runner is not directed at children. It is not part of the App Store Kids category or the Google Play Families program, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights {#rights}

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you,
- have it corrected or deleted,
- restrict or object to its processing,
- receive it in a portable format,
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Because we do not keep a copy of your game data, most requests about it are handled on your device (for example by uninstalling the game). For data held by Apple, Google or the advertising partners, you can also contact them directly. Send any other request to [{{ site.operator.email }}](mailto:{{ site.operator.email }}). We answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live. In Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11., [naih.hu](https://naih.hu), ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu. You can also go to court.

## Security {#security}

The game communicates with the stores and advertising partners over encrypted connections. The save file is validated when loaded. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we design the game to collect as little as possible.

## Changes to this policy {#changes}

If we change how the game handles data (for example by adding cloud saves or online racing), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. We will point out important changes in the game or in the update notes.
